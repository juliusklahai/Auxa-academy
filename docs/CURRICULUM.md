# Curriculum map

Every exercise defines: objective, demonstration (Learn steps + Demo), practice method (wait or timed), assessment (rubric), feedback (explained mistakes, hints), mastery threshold (80% and required passes) and prerequisites.

## Built in Phase 1

| Lesson | Concept | Exercises (mode, passes needed) | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0-1 Meet the keyboard and middle C | posture, 2/3 black-key groups, middle C, finger numbers, high/low | Middle C x4 (wait, 2); Low/middle/high C (wait, 1) | keyboard geography, pitch |
| L0-2 Musical alphabet C to G | letter names, landmarks, C position | C position up and down 1-2-3-4-5 (wait, 2); Find the letters, no labels (wait, 1) | geography, finger technique |
| L0-3 Black keys: sharps | sharp = next key right | C# D# F# G# A# (wait, 1) | geography |
| L0-4 Feel the pulse | steady beat, quarter note, count-in | Eight steady Cs at 60 BPM (timed, 2) | pulse, rhythm |
| L1-1 Reading the treble staff | lines/spaces, G clef, ledger line C | Read and play, no labels (wait, 2) | treble reading |
| L1-2 Half and whole notes | note values, holding | Quarter/half/whole (timed, 1) | rhythm, treble reading |
| L1-3 Bass clef and left hand | F clef, LH C position, mirror fingering | LH C position (wait, 2); Read bass staff, no labels (wait, 1) | bass reading, finger technique |
| L1-4 First melody: Ode to Joy | stepwise melody, dotted rhythm, two 4-bar phrases | Notes first (wait, 1); Performance at 72 BPM (timed, 1) | rhythm, pitch, treble reading |
| L1-5 Two notes at once and first chords | intervals, C/F/G major chords, chord shapes 1-3-5 | Intervals (wait, 2); C, F and G chords (wait, 2); Chords in time (timed, 1) | pitch, geography, rhythm |

Scaffolding fades: key labels and target highlights are removed in reading exercises (a target reappears only after two consecutive misses, as a hint).

## Roadmap (Phases 2 to 5)

- **Level 2** grand staff, 2/4 3/4 4/4, eighths, dotted notes, hands separate then together, contrary/parallel motion, dynamics.
- **Level 3** half/whole steps, major and minor scales, key signatures, thumb passage, arpeggios, legato/staccato, pedaling.
- **Level 4** intervals, triads and inversions, chord symbols, I-IV-V, Alberti and waltz bass, hymn and lead-sheet accompaniment.
- **Level 5** circle of fifths, seventh chords, secondary dominants, syncopation, compound time, triplets, transposition.
- **Level 6** SATB: each voice alone, any pair, full four parts, voice leading, cadences, hymn playing (Choir Studio).
- **Level 7 and 8** advanced technique, interpretation, counterpoint, score reading, collaborative piano, performance simulation. Completion is never presented as professional certification.

Intensive programs (30/60/90/180 days) and daily plans (10 to 90 minutes) build on the competency profile already tracked.

## Play-along mastery pieces

Every piano lesson ends with 1 to 4 original play-along pieces (generated from a seed, so always the same): the app plays the melody, chords and bass, and you play only the keys the lesson taught. For the middle C lesson you play only middle C; for C position only C to G; for left-hand lessons only the bass notes; for chord lessons the chords. Styles: echo (answer at the end of each bar), call (strong beats), tempo (in time with the band), melody (every note of the tune that uses your keys), chords. A lesson is mastered when its core exercises and at least one play-along piece pass at 80%.

## Singing levels

Easy (a semitone away counts, any octave, no restarts, moves on after 3 tries), Standard (right note in any octave, restart after 5 misses), Strict (about 25 cents), Pro coach (only notes within 15 cents count). Chosen in the Voice studio or in any sung lesson.
